# Continuity Is More Than You Think — research seed

**Status:** candidate research framing, not a theory or a set of established axioms.  
**Date:** 2026-10-14.  
**Origin:** dialogue about PResolver, VSlices, TDidacta and transformations between intentions, semantic representations, realizations and evidence.  
**Scope:** conceptual problem statement and falsifiable research directions; no formal theorem or empirical result is claimed.

## Motivating question

Under what conditions can something undergo material transformation while retaining a **recognizable, reconstructible continuity**, possibly across different representations, technologies, actors and domains?

This starts from the intuition that **continuity is not identical to equality or invariance**. That intuition is not sufficient as a definition. A representation can change radically and retain material intent; another can remain visually identical while losing a critical semantic distinction. Which distinctions matter depends on context, purpose, authority and examinable evidence.

Working phrase, **not a theorem or settled definition**:

> Continuity is not what remains unchanged. It is what makes change reconstructible.

The title **“Continuity Is More Than You Think”** is a prospective paper title, not a claim of validated scientific impact or publication.

## Candidate dimensions / questions

The following are prompts for investigation, **not independent or sufficient axioms**:

| Dimension | Question | Counterexample pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | What makes a subject recognizable across transformations? | Equal structure with different identity, or changed structure with conserved identity |
| Relationships | Which dependencies, distinctions or relations must remain reconstructible? | Correct-looking local outputs with lost cross-artifact context |
| Transformation | Which changes are admissible relative to an intention and its scope? | Useful change that breaks a previously material guarantee |
| Provenance | Can we distinguish observed, inherited, inferred, introduced and revised claims? | A correct-looking artifact whose source or inference cannot be established |
| Authority | Who may define, interpret, approve or revise the meaning being preserved? | A tool-generated interpretation treated as a domain owner's decision |
| Evidence | How can continuity claims be examined and challenged? | Matching outputs that conceal semantic divergence |

Additional dimensions might be necessary; existing ones might be dependent, reducible or misplaced. Do not force a fixed six-part ontology.

## Distinctions worth preserving

- **Continuity is contextual:** what must survive may differ by intended use and responsibility.
- **Continuity is potentially directional and partial:** do not assume symmetry, transitivity or a single scalar score.
- **Semantic equivalence, traceability and continuity are not automatically synonyms.**
- **Authority is not execution capacity:** an LLM, compiler or rendering engine may exhibit behavior without becoming the source of meaning.
- **Change can produce evidence that challenges the initial interpretation:** reconstruction is revisable, not a demand to preserve every prior assumption.
- **Documentation is one preservation surface:** continuity must not be equated with the existence of Markdown or another medium.

## Research program: small before compound

1. **Isolate a narrow case.** Specify one intention and one transformable representation, with a material distinction to preserve.
2. **Propose a bounded contract.** Record which claims belong to the source, what the transformation can change, what it cannot infer and what evidence would challenge it.
3. **Construct competing realizations.** Seek cases that produce similar appearances but differ semantically, and cases that differ structurally yet preserve the relevant meaning.
4. **Test the single relationship first.** Do not start with a universal continuity engine, graph or meta-protocol.
5. **Test composition next.** A chain of individually adequate transformations may fail to preserve end-to-end continuity; composition must be examined independently.
6. **Contrast across domains.** Use software/VSlices and pedagogy/TDidacta without assuming their shared terminology implies shared semantics.
7. **Revise, reject or promote.** Preserve counterexamples and decision history. Promotion into PResolver requires independent evaluation and explicit adoption.

## Candidate witnesses

- [VSlices canonical conceptual surface](https://github.com/the-alive-lab/vslices): suite-wide grounded projection, product-owned semantics, provenance and explicit maturity.
- [VSlices Intermediate Representation](https://github.com/the-alive-lab/vslices/blob/main/products/intermediate-representation/README.md): semantic contracts independent of a particular target representation.
- [VSlices Tooling](https://github.com/the-alive-lab/vslices/blob/main/products/tooling/README.md): authoring, validation, lowering, materialization, lineage and explicit failure under missing semantic knowledge.
- [TDidacta conceptual surface](https://github.com/the-alive-lab/tdidacta) and [Platform](https://github.com/tdidacta/platform): candidate contrasting domain for pedagogical intention and realization. Actual mechanisms and guarantees must be reconstructed from their own sources.

These are pointers to possible evidence, **not proof** that a general continuity property has been found.

## Questions left open

- What is the subject of continuity: an artifact, proposition, capability, relation, intention, task, actor or evolving set of them?
- How is loss of meaning detected when a representation cannot express what its source can?
- When are two realizations comparable, and when is comparison itself inappropriate?
- Which continuity properties compose, and under which preconditions?
- Which candidate notions belong to a PResolver foundation versus a flavor or a particular implementation?
- What empirical evaluation could separate this proposal from provenance tracking, traceability, versioning and semantic conformance as already studied?
