# Candidate knowledge

This directory holds concepts and relationships proposed for PResolver but not yet adopted as canonical definitions.

A candidate is not a PResolver axiom, product, capability, implementation requirement or proven generalization. Each candidate should expose its originating cases, responsibility boundaries, alternative interpretations, observations needed to evaluate it, and unresolved questions.

## Current candidates

- [Continuity beyond invariance](continuity-beyond-invariance.md) — inquiry into identity, relations, transformation, provenance, authority and evidence across changes.
- [Capabilities across flavors](capabilities-across-flavors.md) — inquiry into reusable capability identities that may be realized through different flavor products, motivated by VSlices Tooling and TDidacta Platform.

## Promotion boundary

Keep proposed PResolver foundations distinguishable from claims currently owned by VSlices, TDidacta or another flavor. Repeated vocabulary is not enough to establish generality. A specific case may reveal a useful mechanism without making that mechanism universal.

See [the existing PResolver bootstrap](../README.md) and [the supervised transfer study](https://github.com/vslices/research-space/blob/main/lines/cross-flavor-resolution/studies/stu-001-transferencia-tdidacta/12-caso-s-supervisado-presolver-kcollectiva.md). These notes do not supersede either source.
